AI EVALUATION · FEATURED WORK

From tasks to trusted training signals.

A trustworthy result needs a record of what ran, how it was graded, and who reviewed it.

PROBLEM

AI training tasks lose value when no one can explain how they ran, were scored, or got approved.

DESIGN

Built the workflow with React and FastAPI/gRPC, linking sandbox runs, automated grading, human review, and a PostgreSQL audit trail on GKE. Tracing spanned the gateway and eight services.

IMPACT

Each submission gained a traceable quality path. gRPC tracing surfaced silent failures, and CI repairs restored 26 blocked jobs.

System visuals are illustrative. Company interfaces and source code are not shown.

01 / AI EVALUATION✦

Guardrails for AI Training

Problem A task can look right while a broken environment or grader teaches an AI model the wrong lesson.

Design Audited 15 QA runs and specified MCP setup and grading contracts, sandbox jobs, queued execution, and checkpoint regrading.

Impact Mapped 94 ways the environment can fail into a phased QA plan for catching bad training signals early.

EvaluationArchitecture
02 / DATA OPERATIONS✦

The Trusted Data Pipeline

Problem Teams need useful customer data, but reviewers should never need to see the originals.

Design Designed task-based collection, repeatable anonymisation runs, automated QA, and two-stage review with access controls.

Impact The blueprint ties each approval to its anonymisation run and keeps raw data outside the reviewer path.

Workflow designData quality

Skin Insights with Evidence

Problem A product list cannot explain why a skincare recommendation fits or whether its claims are real.

Design Combined AI skin analysis, product matching, and ingredient checks against official brand pages.

Impact Recommendations now explain why a product fits and show the source behind ingredient claims.

From Idea to Factory

Problem A brand name often hides the factory and components behind a product.

Design Mapped seller-to-factory evidence, filtered resellers, broke ideas into components, and drafted exportable build specs.

05 / RECRUITING AI✦

Hiring with Evidence

Problem Recruiters see long candidate lists but too little proof of fit.

Design Built agent components for discovery, source-linked dossiers, role-specific matching, and recruiter inspection.
