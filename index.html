AI EVALUATION · FEATURED WORK

From tasks to trusted training signals.

A trustworthy result needs a record of what ran, how it was graded, and who reviewed it.

PROBLEM

AI training tasks lose value when no one can explain how they ran, were scored, or got approved.

DESIGN

Separated sandbox execution, automated grading, human review, and audit into a service-backed workflow using FastAPI/gRPC, PostgreSQL, and GKE. Sentry tracing crossed the gateway and eight services.

IMPACT

Each accepted task carries execution, score, and approval evidence through the pipeline. Cross-service tracing exposed silent gRPC failures, while CI repairs restored 26 blocked jobs.

System visuals are illustrative. Company interfaces and source code are not shown.

01 / AI EVALUATION✦

Guardrails for AI Training

Problem A task can look right while a broken environment or grader teaches an AI model the wrong lesson.

Design Turned 15 QA audits into a reusable specification for MCP setup and grading contracts, isolated sandbox jobs, queued execution, and checkpoint regrading.

Impact Catalogued 94 environment failure modes into a phased QA plan, making checks consistent across new training tasks.

EvaluationArchitecture
02 / DATA OPERATIONS✦

The Trusted Data Pipeline

Problem Teams need useful customer data, but reviewers should never need to see the originals.

Design Designed an asynchronous data pipeline: direct object-store uploads, isolated AI anonymisation workers, frozen run snapshots, and role-scoped two-stage review.

Impact The architecture keeps large files off app servers and originals away from reviewers, while linking every approval to its exact anonymisation run.

Workflow designData quality
03 / CONSUMER AI✦

Skin Insights with Evidence

Problem A product list cannot explain why a skincare recommendation fits or whether its claims are real.

Design Separated FastAPI skin analysis and matching from queued product-research workers that verify ingredients against official sources and store claim provenance.

Impact Recommendations explain fit with traceable product evidence; unsupported claims stay unverified, and slow research stays off the customer request path.

04 / INDUSTRIAL AI✦

From Idea to Factory

Problem A brand name often hides the factory and components behind a product.

Design Specified an agent workflow to verify factories behind sellers, map components and suppliers, and export an evidence-linked build specification.

Impact The design creates an inspectable route from rough idea to supplier shortlist and build plan; the product remains in development.

05 / RECRUITING AI✦

Hiring with Evidence

Problem Recruiters see long candidate lists but too little proof of fit.

Design Built modular agents for candidate discovery, cited dossiers, role matching, and evaluation, with provider adapters and bounded runs.

Impact Six merged agent components and 54 passing offline tests established a reusable sourcing base; production match quality and throughput remain under validation.

06 / AI GOVERNANCE✦

Can an AI Really Forget?

Problem Removing training data does not prove a model has forgotten it.

Design Built a method-agnostic audit with pluggable recovery probes and evidence scoring; designed a separate control plane for data lineage and erasure holds.

Impact Compatible model checkpoints can be challenged through the same evaluation path; the prototype reports inconclusive when evidence cannot support a deletion claim.
